# InmoData Marketing Brief

## Purpose of this document

This is the working brand and product brief for InmoData marketing. It is designed for a marketer, copywriter, social media manager, or AI assistant creating posts, ad copy, campaign ideas, landing-page copy, and creative direction.

Use Spanish for public-facing content unless a campaign specifically targets an English-speaking audience in the Dominican Republic.

## Brand summary

**Brand:** InmoData  
**Category:** Real-estate data and decision-support platform  
**Market:** Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic, expanding over time  
**Website:** https://inmodata.onrender.com  
**Primary promise:** Help people understand the published price of an apartment before they make a decision.

InmoData is a free public tool for exploring apartment listings, comparing price per square meter, reviewing sector-level market information, estimating mortgage payments, and identifying listings that may be priced below comparable properties.

It is not a broker, a bank, a real-estate agency, a legal advisor, or a certified appraiser.

## The problem InmoData solves

People looking for an apartment in Santo Domingo often see a price but cannot easily tell whether it is reasonable for the sector, size, and characteristics of the property. Listings may use different areas, prices, currencies, and levels of detail. That makes comparison difficult.

InmoData turns public listing information into simple, practical comparisons:

- Price per square meter.
- Market references by sector.
- Property-versus-property comparison.
- Possible below-market opportunities.
- Price-drop tracking when the same listing is observed over time.
- A mortgage-payment estimate.
- Data freshness and coverage information.

## Primary audience

### Main audience

People in the Dominican Republic, especially Santo Domingo, who are considering buying an apartment and want to make a better-informed decision.

Typical situations:

- A buyer sees an apartment online and asks, “Is this price fair?”
- A first-time buyer needs a simple comparison before talking to an agent or bank.
- A family is comparing two apartments with different sizes and prices.
- An investor wants to identify lower price-per-square-meter listings.
- A buyer wants to estimate a monthly financing payment before applying.

### Secondary audience

- Real-estate agents and brokers who want a quick market-reference tool.
- Small investors evaluating neighborhoods.
- Developers and marketers researching published apartment prices.
- Journalists, creators, and educators discussing the Santo Domingo housing market.

## Product positioning

InmoData should feel like a clear, independent, data-first companion for a real-estate decision.

Recommended positioning sentence:

> InmoData helps you compare published apartment prices in Santo Domingo using price-per-square-meter data and sector-level market references.

Short version:

> Datos para entender el precio de un apartamento.

## Core messages

Use these ideas repeatedly, in different wording:

1. Do not evaluate an apartment only by its total price; compare the price per square meter.
2. A listing can look cheap or expensive until you compare it with similar properties in the same sector.
3. InmoData makes the first research step simpler and free.
4. Data is a guide for asking better questions, not a replacement for a professional appraisal or legal review.
5. The tool is made for Dominican Republic real-estate decisions, beginning with Santo Domingo.

## How InmoData calculates a market reference

Use simple language when explaining the product:

- **Price per square meter:** published sale price divided by construction area.
- **Market reference:** the median price per square meter of comparable active apartments in the same sector.
- **Below market:** price per square meter at least 10% below the reference.
- **Within market range:** within 10% below or above the reference.
- **Above market:** more than 10% above the reference.
- **Comparable listings:** InmoData prioritizes apartments with a similar area and, when possible, the same bedroom count.
- **Minimum comparison rule:** the product should not produce a market valuation unless it has at least three comparable active listings.
- **Small sample:** fewer than five comparables is displayed as an indicative, lower-confidence sample.
- **Freshness:** a listing is marked as pending an update when it has not been seen in the previous 14 days.

## Truth and compliance rules

These rules are important for all marketing and AI-generated content.

### Safe claims

- “Compare published apartment prices.”
- “Explore price-per-square-meter data.”
- “Use data as a starting point for your property research.”
- “Find listings that may be below the local market reference.”
- “Estimate a possible mortgage payment.”
- “Review market information by sector.”
- “Free to explore.”

### Do not claim

- “InmoData tells you the exact value of a property.”
- “InmoData guarantees a good investment.”
- “This is a certified appraisal.”
- “This is financial, legal, tax, or investment advice.”
- “Every listing is available.”
- “The published price is the final sale price.”
- “The cheapest listing is automatically the best deal.”
- “Save money guaranteed.”
- “The entire Dominican Republic is covered.”

### Required qualification for price-related ads

When an ad mentions whether a price is fair, cheap, below market, or above market, include a short qualification in the caption, landing page, or final creative frame:

> Resultados orientativos basados en anuncios publicados y propiedades comparables. No sustituyen una tasación, inspección técnica o revisión legal.

### Data count rule

Never hard-code a listing total or number of sectors in a post unless it was checked on the same day at https://inmodata.onrender.com/datos/.

The platform is refreshed over time, so counts change. Use wording such as “propiedades activas disponibles” unless a current count is confirmed.

## Brand personality and voice

### Personality

- Clear and calm.
- Practical and data-led.
- Helpful, not salesy.
- Modern without sounding technical or cold.
- Honest about limitations.
- Local to the Dominican Republic.

### Voice

- Use clear Dominican Spanish that is understandable across Latin America.
- Prefer short, direct sentences.
- Explain real-estate terms in plain language.
- Use “tú” for social posts and consumer advertising.
- Use “InmoData” as the subject, not “we,” when explaining a calculation.
- Be confident without overpromising.

### Preferred language

- “Precio por m²” instead of overly technical wording.
- “Sector” instead of “zona” when discussing the product filters.
- “Apartamento” as the primary property type in current campaigns.
- “Precio publicado” when referring to listing prices.
- “Referencia de mercado” rather than “valor garantizado.”
- “Oportunidad para investigar” rather than “ganga garantizada.”

### Avoid

- Alarmist language: “No te dejes engañar.”
- Shame-based language: “Estás pagando de más.”
- Financial promises: “Hazte rico,” “rentabilidad garantizada,” or “inversión segura.”
- Unsupported superlatives: “La mejor plataforma,” “la única app,” or “la fuente más precisa.”
- Complex appraisal jargon without an explanation.

## Visual identity

### Overall look

The website has a dark, modern, data-dashboard appearance. It does not rely on property photography or decorative imagery. The interface uses generous spacing, dark surfaces, white text, cyan highlights, and simple tables and cards.

Marketing creative should feel like a clean data product, not a traditional real-estate flyer.

### Color application rules

- Use dark navy as the dominant background.
- Use cyan sparingly as the attention color: numbers, buttons, underlines, labels, and the InmoData wordmark.
- Keep body text white or light gray for readability.
- Use green, amber, and red only to communicate a data meaning. Do not use them only as decoration.
- Avoid bright multicolor designs, gold “luxury” palettes, or crowded property-catalog layouts.

### Typography

The website uses a clean system sans-serif stack:

```text
Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif
```

For marketing creatives, use a modern sans-serif with a similar clear style. Good choices include Inter, Arial, Helvetica, DM Sans, or Manrope.

Typography direction:

- Headline: bold, large, direct.
- Supporting copy: regular weight, high contrast.
- Data: bold numbers with cyan emphasis.
- Labels: uppercase, small, letter-spaced, cyan.
- Avoid script fonts, serif luxury fonts, and handwritten fonts.

### Layout style

- Large headline at the top or left.
- One core idea per post or ad.
- Use cards, comparison rows, simple tables, and large price-per-square-meter figures.
- Leave strong empty space around text.
- Use a clear cyan call-to-action button or link label.
- Keep mobile readability as the priority.

### Image policy

The product experience currently does not use property images. Marketing can also work without images by using:

- Data cards.
- Before-and-after price comparisons.
- Large “US$/m²” figures.
- Simple map-like sector labels without making a detailed map claim.
- Clean UI screenshots, when current and readable.
- Text-led carousels.

Do not use random luxury-home photos that imply a specific listing is part of InmoData. If property imagery is used later, it must be licensed or explicitly permitted and clearly separated from data claims.

## Suggested content pillars

### 1. Understand the price

Teach that total price alone is not enough. Explain price per square meter with short examples.

Example hook:

> Dos apartamentos pueden costar US$200,000. Eso no significa que tengan el mismo valor por m².

### 2. Sector comparisons

Show that location changes the price reference. Use actual live sector data only after checking the website on publication day.

Example hook:

> Antes de decidir entre dos sectores, compara el precio por m².

### 3. Buyer education

Create checklists for viewing an apartment: price per m², availability, documents, condition, maintenance, financing, and comparables.

Example hook:

> Antes de separar un apartamento, revisa estas 5 cosas.

### 4. Financing clarity

Use the mortgage calculator to explain down payment, term, interest, and estimated monthly payment.

Example hook:

> El precio de compra no es lo único que debes calcular. También importa tu cuota mensual.

### 5. Data transparency

Explain sample size, data freshness, and why InmoData labels results as orientative.

Example hook:

> Un buen dato también explica sus límites.

### 6. Product demonstrations

Short screen recordings or static UI walkthroughs: enter sector, price, and area; view result; link to the tool.

Example hook:

> ¿Te enviaron el precio de un apartamento? Revísalo en menos de un minuto.

## Recommended campaign structure

### Campaign 1: Awareness

**Objective:** Introduce the problem: buyers need more than a total price.  
**Audience:** Adults interested in real estate, apartments, mortgages, investing, and Santo Domingo.  
**Creative:** Text-led data card or short UI demonstration.  
**Landing page:** Homepage or valuation tool.

### Campaign 2: Tool usage

**Objective:** Get users to try the valuation or comparison tool.  
**Creative:** “Enter sector + price + m²” three-step demonstration.  
**Landing page:** https://inmodata.onrender.com/mercado/valorar/

### Campaign 3: Retargeting

**Objective:** Bring site visitors back to explore listings or financing.  
**Creative:** “Compare before deciding” or “Estimate your possible payment.”  
**Landing page:** Comparison tool or mortgage calculator.

Do not set up retargeting or conversion tracking until the privacy policy and consent requirements are reviewed and updated for the chosen advertising and analytics tools.

## Pre-publication checklist

Before publishing any post or advertisement, confirm:

- The destination URL works on mobile.
- Any listing count, sector count, price, or market figure was checked the same day.
- The campaign does not promise an appraisal, investment return, loan approval, or savings.
- Price-related content includes the orientative-results qualification.
- The creative uses the approved dark navy, cyan, white, and gray visual system.
- The headline is easy to understand without real-estate experience.
- The call to action leads to the most relevant InmoData tool.
- Any analytics, pixels, or retargeting tools are cleared with the current privacy-policy and consent requirements before activation.

## Current product limitations to communicate honestly

- InmoData currently begins with apartment data and sector coverage in Santo Domingo.
- Coverage varies by sector and changes as data is refreshed.
- Some sectors may not yet have enough comparable listings for a valuation.
- Published prices may differ from final negotiated sale prices.
- Availability must be confirmed with the original listing source.
- The mortgage tool is an estimate, not a lender offer.
